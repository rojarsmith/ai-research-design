# Operating a US company while living in Taiwan

Reviewed on October 11, 2026. The complete operating guide is in Traditional Chinese, the language requested for this research. This overview provides an English entry point.

The main scenario is a Taiwan tax resident who is neither a US citizen nor a green-card holder and works primarily from Taiwan. US tax residency must still be tested against actual presence. Compare legal entity structure, federal tax classification, state nexus, and Taiwan obligations separately.

The guide covers formation choices, preparation, first-year and recurring costs, annual deadlines, banking, owner transactions, contracts, intellectual property, hiring, expansion, and closure. It explains why zero sales may still trigger information reporting and why a US customer or US bank account does not alone determine the source of service income.

Private company records and case assessments are excluded from this directory and kept outside the Git workspace. Public research does not contain tax identifiers, personal addresses, account numbers, credentials, private transactions, or paths to private records.

See the [full guide and document index](README.zh-TW.md). Its linked research chapters are written in Traditional Chinese; this English page is an overview, not a full translation.
