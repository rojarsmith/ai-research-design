# Online dating for marriage and family planning

[完整繁體中文研究](README.zh-TW.md)

Research checked on October 12, 2026, Taiwan time. This topic examines online dating for an unmarried man in his forties living in Kaohsiung who wants to meet adult women under 35 and pursue marriage and children, including long-distance and international relationships.

The Traditional Chinese report is the complete version. It includes platform comparisons, profile and conversation examples, a 30-day trial plan, international dating considerations, safety guidance, a source register, and the research log. Platform recommendations are provisional workflow choices, not measured success-rate rankings.
